Campus News
January 10, 2022
Around Campus
Experts in the news

Dr. Bradley W. Hart, associate professor, Media, Communications and Journalism, was mentioned in a story on how China Peak was named.

The story appeared in The Fresno Bee (Dec. 31) How did China Peak get its name? Here’s the story of the honor given to a Chinese cowboy

Dr. Andrew Fiala, professor, philosophy and director of The Ethics Center, wrote an article about how humanity has learned to endure pandemics throughout history.

The story appeared in The Fresno Bee (Dec. 18) Fresno State professor: From Black Death to COVID, societies learn to endure pandemics

Dr. Malisa Lee, associate vice president for enrollment services, was quoted on making the college application process simpler for students.

The story aired on ABC 30 (Dec. 10) Fresno State simplifies college application process

Dr. Janine Nkosi, sociology lecturer, was quoted on the City of Fresno receiving about $171 million in ARPA funding and how to spend the remainder of the funding for low-income families.

The story appeared in The Fresno Bee (Dec. 10) Fresnans demand COVID relief money go to housing protections. Did City Council listen?

Dr. Tania Pacheco-Werner, co-assistant director, Central Valley Health Policy Institute, was quoted on the requirement for medium- and heavy-duty trucks and busses to get regularly smog-checked.

The story appeared in Phys.org (Dec. 10) California's heavy-duty trucks don't have to get smog-checked. Why that's about to change

Dr. Keith Putirka, professor, Earth and Environmental Sciences was quoted in an article on volcanic hot spots.

The story appeared in Science News (Jan. 6) Some volcanic hot spots may have a surprisingly shallow heat source

Dr. Malik Simba, professor emeritus, Africana Studies and history, wrote an article about the Kyle Rittenhosue verdict.

The story appeared in The Fresno Bee (Dec. 12) Fresno State professor explains differing verdicts in the Rittenhouse, Arbery cases

Seng Alex Vang, lecturer in Asian American Studies, was quoted on the cultural rituals, family gatherings and courtship during the Hmong New Year celebration.

The story appeared on PBS News Hour (Dec. 29) In face of pandemic, Hmong Americans press forward to maintain New Year traditions
