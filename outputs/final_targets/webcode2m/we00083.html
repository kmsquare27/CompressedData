Price Breakdown

Ramp Up Class

PRICETYPE OF TRAININGDETAILS
$150M-W-F, 12 sessionsThe “Ramp Up” program is geared towards healthy individuals who are new to our training methods. This is a 4-week series of 12 classes that will focus on teaching you the movements most necessary for entry into our ongoing group class system. Each class will include a warm up, new exercise orientation, exercise/skill review, and a workout. At the end of the Ramp Up program, each athlete will be presented with options to continue training based on their level of fitness, as well as their ability to perform the movements safely and effectively. Options may include level 1 classes, level 2 classes, level 3 classes, or private training.
