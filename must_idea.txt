 Inductive Bias Transfer
This is more speculative but potentially impactful.

Hypothesis: Training on compressed HTML forces the model to learn "rendering-aware" representations — it must predict the visual output from a shorter input, which may induce better visual understanding.

Experiment:

Pre-train a vision-language model on (screenshot, compressed HTML)

Fine-tune on downstream tasks (UI classification, element detection, layout understanding)

Compare against pre-training on (screenshot, original HTML)

The compression acts as a task-relevant regularizer — the model learns to ignore visual-irrelevant tokens.
